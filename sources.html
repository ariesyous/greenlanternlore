How to use the references

Issue references identify the series and publication year when titles repeat: Green Lantern (2005) #1 is a different book from Green Lantern (2021) #1. A cover date can differ from the day a comic went on sale; the reading guide orders stories and labels publication spans rather than treating those dates as events inside the fiction.

Sources beside the revised guides link to DC's issue listings, collected editions, and editorial retrospectives. Issue numbers support the reading sequence; publisher descriptions support the stated premise and collection contents. Commentary about a story's meaning is our interpretation. A bibliography is a starting point, not proof of every sentence in an older article.

New and revised guides separate a premise from optional plot outcomes, identify continuity, and mark their review date. Character histories are closed by default. Reading lists and character affiliations can still reveal that a character participates in a later story. For the distinctions between an original story, a later explanation, and a separate universe, see Which Green Lantern continuity?

Collection titles are reading references, not a promise of current stock or subscription availability. Compare the issue list in your edition; special issues and annuals are sometimes collected separately. The longer checklist is a selected route through the Lantern books, not every DC crossover appearance.

Editorial guidance reviewed November 4, 2026. Older profiles are being revised in stages; a profile without source links has not received the same citation pass. Suggest a correction with the page, series, issue, and relevant scene.

Start with a sourced reading route

  • Far Sector — Jo Mullein, the City Enduring, and a complete twelve-issue mystery. DC collection.
  • Secret Origin — Hal's origin, Green Lantern (2005) #29–35. DC collection.
  • War Journal — John Stewart's twelve-issue solo series. DC's final-issue listing.
  • Hard-Traveling Heroes — the classic Hal Jordan and Oliver Queen collaboration, with edition-specific issue guidance.
  • Green Lantern (2021) — John, Jo, and the Corps between the Morrison and 2023 runs.
  • Parallax and Ion — identity histories distinguished from the later emotional-entity mythology.

Art credits

All artwork is credited on the page where it appears — comic covers, character portraits, entity art, and adaptation stills. Every image is self-hosted; nothing is hotlinked. Sources include DC Comics, Wallpapercat, Wallpapercave, ScreenRant, CBR, ComicsBeat, Pinterest, and credited fan artists.
