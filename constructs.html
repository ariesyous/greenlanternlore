The Boxing Glove

The most famous construct in comics. Hal's go-to: a giant green fist that hits like a truck. As the man himself puts it — "just takes a little imagination."

Rage Plasma

Red Lanterns don't make constructs. They vomit corrosive rage-plasma that burns through anything — including, occasionally, their own allies' patience.

How constructs work

Every construct is limited by two things: the wielder's imagination and their emotional control. Hal Jordan thinks in blunt instruments. John Stewart thinks in blueprints. Kyle Rayner thinks in illustrations. Sinestro thinks in your worst nightmares. The ring doesn't choose the weapon — the mind does.
