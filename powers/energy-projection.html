Powers of the Ring

Energy Projection

Raw emotional light — beams, bolts, and shockwaves.

The power

Raw blasts of emotional light — beams, bolts, and shockwaves powerful enough to shatter asteroids or stagger beings like Superman. The output scales with the wielder's emotional intensity.

Sinestro wields yellow fear energy like a scalpel, precise and terrifying. Hal Jordan's green blasts are raw power backed by unbreakable will. The stronger the emotion, the stronger the blast.

Energy projection is the most direct application of the ring — no imagination required, just feeling. But without control, it's just destruction. The best Lanterns use it sparingly, preferring constructs for their versatility.
