Powers of the Ring

Energy Constructs

Thought made solid — the signature power of the ring.

The power

The signature power. A ring turns thought into solid light — anything the wielder can imagine and hold in their mind: giant fists, cages, bridges, weapons, entire machines. Imagination shapes the result, but concentration, available charge, and opposing force can limit what the construct achieves.

Hal Jordan is famous for the boxing glove — a giant green fist that hits like a truck. John Stewart, the architect, builds with an engineer's precision: intricate machines, working weapons, entire structures. Kyle Rayner, the artist, draws on his artistic imagination for varied forms.

Construct use varies across the factions and their members. Sinestro Corps members forge blades and weapons designed to terrify. Red Lanterns shape their rage into claws and infernos. The constructs are as individual as the minds behind them.
