Emotional Entity

Ion

The whale-like embodiment of willpower — the green light given life.

Willpower · Green light

First appearance: Green Lantern #142 (2001)

Aligned Corps: Green Lantern Corps

The story

Every color of the emotional spectrum has a living embodiment — a creature born from the emotion itself. Ion is willpower's: a vast, serene, whale-like being that drifts through the cosmos, drawn to those whose will burns brightest.

Ion is not a ruler or a weapon. It is a symbiote of sorts, bonding with hosts it deems worthy and amplifying their will to impossible heights. Kyle Rayner was its first great host, carrying Ion during the Corps' darkest rebuilding years. Later it bonded with Sodam Yat, the prophesied Lantern of Daxam.

Where Parallax feeds on fear and corrupts, Ion steadies and elevates. To host Ion is to have your willpower confirmed by the universe itself — the green light saying, in effect: yes, you are enough.
