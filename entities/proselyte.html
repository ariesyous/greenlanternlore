Emotional Entity

Proselyte

The cephalopod embodiment of compassion — the light that feels all pain.

Compassion · Indigo light

First appearance: Green Lantern #52 (2010)

Aligned Corps: Indigo Tribe

The story

Compassion's entity is Proselyte, a vast cephalopod-like being that drifts through the dark between stars, drawn to suffering the way a moth is drawn to flame. It was born from the first act of mercy — the moment one being chose to ease another's pain at cost to itself.

Proselyte does not distinguish between the deserving and the undeserving; compassion doesn't. Its hosts are flooded with the pain of everyone around them, and given the power to take that pain away. It is a heavy gift — to feel everything, and to be compelled to heal it.

The Indigo Tribe, whose rings force compassion on the remorseless, regard Proselyte with something between worship and fear.
