Emotional Entity

Parallax

The insectile embodiment of fear — the yellow light's living nightmare.

Fear · Yellow light

First appearance: Green Lantern #50 (1994)

Aligned Corps: Sinestro Corps

The story

Before it was a name whispered in dread, Parallax was simply fear itself — an ancient, insectile entity born from the first living thing that ever felt terror. The Guardians of the Universe trapped it inside the Green Lantern Central Power Battery, and its presence there created the yellow impurity: the reason green rings once failed against the color yellow.

Parallax is a parasite of the mind. It found its perfect host in Hal Jordan at his lowest moment — grieving, broken, furious at the Guardians — and wore him like a suit through the destruction of the Corps in Emerald Twilight. For years the universe believed Hal had simply fallen. The truth was worse, and more tragic: fear had been driving.

Even freed from Hal, Parallax remains the spectrum's great corruptor. Wherever it goes, courage curdles into terror — and the yellow light follows.
