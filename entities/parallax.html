Emotional Entity · Mainstream comics

Parallax

Fear given form—and a name whose meaning changed with Green Lantern history.

Fear · Yellow light

Hal's identity: Green Lantern (1990) #50 (1994)

Entity revelation: Green Lantern: Rebirth #3 (2004–05 series)

Related Corps: Sinestro Corps

Spoiler boundary: the explanation below reveals the central retcon in Green Lantern: Rebirth and a major development in Sinestro Corps War.

One name, two publication milestones

In Emerald Twilight, Hal Jordan destroys the Corps and emerges as Parallax. Readers in 1994 encountered a fallen hero using a new identity. They were not yet being shown the separate, insectlike fear entity of later stories.

Geoff Johns and Ethan Van Sciver's Green Lantern: Rebirth reinterprets that history: Parallax is an ancient being that infected Hal. The distinction matters when reading older comics. Hal's actions remain part of the story, but the explanation for his fall was added a decade later. DC's retrospective explicitly describes this later revelation in its account of Kyle Rayner's debut era.

Why green rings once failed against yellow

The Guardians imprisoned Parallax inside the Central Power Battery. Its presence contaminated the green power supply: the yellow impurity was fear embedded in the Corps' own source of power. Rebirth #3 connects the old color weakness to that imprisonment and to the need to confront fear. DC's official history timeline cites that issue for the explanation.

This is a later explanation of an older rule, not evidence that every yellow object contains Parallax. Nor is wearing a yellow ring the same as hosting the entity: a Sinestro Corps member channels fear through a ring; a host carries Parallax itself.

Spoilers — hosts and outcomes in Rebirth and Sinestro Corps War

Hal Jordan is the defining host in Rebirth. Kyle Rayner becomes another during Sinestro Corps War, making the fear entity a personal threat to the Lantern who carried the Corps' legacy after Hal's fall. DC's 2007 Ion one-shot synopsis identifies Kyle's possession and the question of who will next bear Ion.

Ion represents willpower; Parallax represents fear. Their opposition gives the spectrum a conflict that can be fought within a person as well as between armies.

Read the change in context

  1. Green Lantern (1990) #48–50 — Hal's fall and the original Parallax identity.
  2. Green Lantern: Rebirth #1–6 — the fear-entity explanation, especially #3.
  3. Sinestro Corps War — begin with Green Lantern: Sinestro Corps Special #1 (2007), then follow the crossover order.
