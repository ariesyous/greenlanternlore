Emotional Entity

The Life Entity

The embodiment of life itself — the white light that began everything.

Life · White light

First appearance: Blackest Night #7 (2010)

Aligned Corps: White Lantern Corps

The story

Before the spectrum, before the Corps, before emotion had colors — there was life. The Life Entity is the first living thing, the spark from which all living things descend, and the white light is its radiance. Every other entity is, in a sense, its child.

The Entity slept hidden on Earth for eons, buried where life began. Nekron and the Black Lanterns sought it above all else: to kill the embodiment of life would be to unmake living things everywhere. Its defenders — the White Lanterns, chosen in the Blackest Night's final hour — carried the white light not as conquerors but as vessels.

It does not speak in words. It does not need to. It is the reason there is anyone to speak at all.
