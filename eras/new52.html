The New 52 Era

The universe reboots; the Corps starts over.

2011 – 2015

Start with Green Lantern (vol. 5) #1.

The story

In 2011, the New 52 relaunched DC's titles with new first issues, but Green Lantern retained the core of Geoff Johns' ongoing story. Following War of the Green Lanterns, Hal Jordan was expelled from the Corps and Sinestro became a Green Lantern again. The new numbering marks a publishing relaunch, not a fresh origin for the Corps.

It was a messy, fascinating era. Geoff Johns closed out his run with the Third Army and the First Lantern sagas, then handed the reins to a new generation of writers. The Red Lanterns got their own ongoing series — with Guy Gardner, of all people, leading it. Sinestro headlined a solo book that asked whether the universe's greatest tyrant could be its savior.

What to know going in: the opening arcs continue Johns' earlier run, so War of the Green Lanterns provides useful context. DC's continuity explainer describes why Green Lantern and Batman were exceptions to the broader reset.
