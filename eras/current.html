The Current Era

Hal comes home; John takes the spotlight. The saga continues monthly.

2023 – present

Start with Green Lantern (2023) #1.

The story

The current era split the franchise in two — and both halves sing. Jeremy Adams' Green Lantern brings Hal Jordan home to Earth, depowered and rebuilding his life in Coast City, in a back-to-basics run about what the ring means when you're just a man.

Meanwhile Green Lantern: War Journal gives John Stewart the solo spotlight he's always deserved: a cosmic epic about John's family, his past, and a threat that follows him home. It's the most ambitious John Stewart story ever told.

Both books are ongoing monthly — this is the living edge of the mythos, and the easiest place to start reading right now.
