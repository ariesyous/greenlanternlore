2023 – present

Start with Green Lantern (2023) #1.

The story

The 2023 relaunch offers two different starting points. Jeremy Adams' Green Lantern brings Hal Jordan home to Earth, rebuilding his life in Coast City and exploring what being a Lantern means outside the Corps.

Green Lantern: War Journal, by Phillip Kennedy Johnson and Montos, gives John Stewart a solo story about his family, his past, and a cosmic threat that follows him home. The series concluded with #12 in August 2024.

Start with Green Lantern (2023) #1 for Hal, or read the complete twelve-issue War Journal for John. DC's final-issue listing confirms War Journal's conclusion. Publication status reviewed November 4, 2026.
