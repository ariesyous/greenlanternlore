Secret Origin

Hal Jordan's origin, retold.

2008

Collected in Green Lantern: Secret Origin.

The story

Every hero gets their origin retold eventually — this is Hal's definitive version. The test pilot, the crash, Abin Sur's dying wish, and the choice that made him a Lantern.

Reads fine on its own, but it also quietly recontextualizes everything: Sinestro's role in Hal's early days, the Guardians' secrets, and the first hints of the larger saga. A breather between wars that deepens both.
