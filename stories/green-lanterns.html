The story

While Hal and the Corps fought cosmic wars, Jessica Cruz and Simon Baz held down the toughest beat in the universe: Earth. Sam Humphries' series is the most human Green Lantern book in years — Jessica battling crippling anxiety that once kept her from leaving her apartment, Simon navigating life as a Muslim-American hero under suspicion.

Their partnership is the heart of it: two rookies who barely trust each other, forced to share a sector and a destiny. The early arcs pit them against the Red Lanterns and the Phantom Lantern — a rogue Guardian's weapon that can channel the entire emotional spectrum.

The perfect entry point for new readers, and the series that made Jessica Cruz a fan-favorite Lantern.
