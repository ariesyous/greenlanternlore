The story

For a decade, Hal Jordan was dead — a villain, then a sacrifice, then a ghost. Green Lantern: Rebirth brings him back, and in doing so rebuilds the entire mythology from the foundation up.

This is the perfect entry point: it re-establishes who Hal is, what the Corps means, and — crucially — it reveals the truth about Parallax and the yellow impurity. Everything Geoff Johns builds for the next seven years starts here.

Six issues. No prior knowledge required. Start here.
