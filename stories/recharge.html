The story

With Hal back, the Corps itself needs rebuilding — and Recharge is that story. New recruits, old veterans, and the machinery of the Corps coming back to life.

It introduces the next generation of Lanterns and re-establishes Oa, the Guardians, and the Corps as a functioning institution. Essential connective tissue between Rebirth and the war to come.
