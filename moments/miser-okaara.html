The Miser of Okaara

Larfleeze claims the orange light — and refuses to share.

Long ago

The story

The orange light of avarice could have powered an army. Instead it powers one being: Larfleeze, who found it, claimed it, and has refused — for the rest of time — to share it with anyone.

The Guardians, who wanted no war over the Vega system, made a pact: leave Larfleeze and his hoard alone, and he'll stay on Okaara. It's held for eons. Mostly.
