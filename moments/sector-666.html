The Massacre of Sector 666

The Manhunters slaughter a sector. Five survive.

Eons ago

The story

Before there were Lanterns, there were Manhunters — and the Manhunters decided, with machine logic, that all life was the problem. In Sector 666 they carried that logic to its end: every living thing, slaughtered.

Five beings survived. One of them was Atrocitus. He nursed his rage across the ages — through empires rising and falling — until the red light of anger answered him, and he forged it into a Corps of his own.

The Guardians buried the massacre. But the universe doesn't forget, and neither does rage. Everything the Red Lanterns are flows from this single wound.
