Fear Organizes

Sinestro forges the yellow light into a mirror of the Corps.

The modern age

The story

Sinestro was the Green Lantern Corps' greatest hero — and its greatest failure. Exiled for ruling his sector as a tyrant, he didn't repent. He concluded the Guardians were wrong about everything.

If willpower could police the universe, fear could do it better. He forged the yellow light — the Corps' ancient weakness — into a mirror of everything he once served: sectors, a central battery on Qward, and an oath of terror.

The Sinestro Corps War wasn't a grudge. It was a philosophy with an army: order through fear, against freedom through will.
