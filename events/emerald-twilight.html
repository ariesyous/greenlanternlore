1994

Emerald Twilight

The fall of the greatest Green Lantern.

The story

Hal Jordan has always been the Green Lantern Corps' greatest hero — fearless to a fault, the man the Guardians themselves called the greatest of them all. Then his hometown, Coast City, is annihilated in an instant, seven million lives gone. Broken by grief, Hal begs the Guardians for the power to undo it. They refuse.

What follows is the darkest chapter in Corps history. Hal drains the Central Power Battery, cuts a path through Oa, and kills his fellow Lanterns — Kilowog, Sinestro, and wave after wave of the Corps — before striking down nearly every Guardian. He emerges as something new and terrible: Parallax.

Only one Guardian survives: Ganthet, who escapes with the last power ring and entrusts it to a young freelance artist named Kyle Rayner. The Corps is gone. The age of Hal Jordan is over — and the age of the last Green Lantern has begun.

Aftermath

The Corps is extinct and Kyle Rayner carries the last ring alone for years. Hal's fall sets up Zero Hour, and his long road to redemption — from Parallax back to hero — becomes one of comics' great second acts.
