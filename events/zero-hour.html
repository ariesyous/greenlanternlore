1994

Zero Hour: Crisis in Time!

Parallax tries to remake the universe.

IssuesZero Hour: Crisis in Time! #4–0
PublishedSeptember 1994
WriterDan Jurgens
ArtistDan Jurgens, Jerry Ordway (inks)
SourcesPublication details via Wikipedia

Continuity note: The direct sequel to Emerald Twilight — Hal Jordan's story as Parallax concludes here. The five issues are numbered #4 to #0 in reverse, counting down to zero.

The story

Hal Jordan has fallen. As Parallax, the former Green Lantern now wields godlike power — and he intends to use it to unmake the universe and rebuild it free of tragedy, free of Coast City's ashes, free of his own sins.

Time itself begins to unravel. Heroes across the DC Universe feel reality shifting as Parallax, allied with the time-manipulating villain Extant, erases history from the future backward. The issues count down from #4 to #0 as the countdown to zero hour approaches.

In the end, the heroes stop Parallax from imposing his vision — but the universe is reborn with subtle differences, and DC's continuity is reshaped. The event closes the book on Hal Jordan's fall and sets the stage for Kyle Rayner's era as the last Green Lantern.

Aftermath

The Corps is extinct and Kyle Rayner carries the last ring alone for years. Hal's fall sets up Zero Hour, and his long road to redemption — from Parallax back to hero — becomes one of comics' great second acts.
