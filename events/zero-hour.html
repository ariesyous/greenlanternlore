1994

Zero Hour: Crisis in Time!

History is unraveling, and the heroes are running out of time.

Start here without the ending: The premise and reading plan below avoid the resolution. Plot history and later continuity changes are in a closed section.

A DC-wide continuity event after Crisis on Infinite Earths. For Lantern readers it continues the consequences of Emerald Twilight. It is not Hal’s final appearance as Parallax.

Why read it?

People and events from incompatible histories begin appearing together as time collapses. The heroes investigate a threat that affects the past and future at once. Extant is the visible enemy early in the story; the larger plan emerges as the countdown proceeds.

Watch for: the competing ideas of repairing history and controlling it. This is useful if you want the 1990s Hal/Kyle transition or the wider DC timeline. It is less direct as a first Green Lantern book because much of the cast comes from other series.

Before, during and after

  • Before — recommended: Green Lantern (1990) #48–50, “Emerald Twilight.” The earlier Armageddon 2001 event supplies Extant’s background; it is optional context for a Lantern-focused reading.
  • Core: #4, #3, #2, #1, then #0. The reverse numbering is intentional; do not begin with #0.
  • Optional Lantern branches: Green Lantern (1990) #55 and #0; Guy Gardner: Warrior #24; Darkstars #24. Follow the omnibus arrangement if combining the tie-ins.
  • Next — Kyle: continue his 1990s Green Lantern run. Next — Hal: The Final Night (1996), then the later Rebirth reinterpretation.

The 2024 Zero Hour 30th Anniversary Special is a later revisit, not a missing sixth chapter of the 1994 series. DC’s anniversary-special description identifies that distinction.

Story history and aftermath — major spoilers

The reveal and the reset

Parallax is revealed as the force working with Extant to remake the timeline. Hal wants to restore what was lost, but his plan would replace everyone else’s history with his own design. Oliver Queen helps stop him, and Damage provides the spark that restarts the universe.

The reset changes parts of continuity rather than erasing every prior adventure. The Justice Society suffers severe losses, and the Legion of Super-Heroes receives a new beginning. DC’s subsequent “Zero Month” issues supply fresh starting points. DC’s Zero Hour explainer distinguishes the story’s resolution from these publishing consequences.

Hal’s story continues

Defeat here does not conclude Hal’s Parallax period. The Final Night later places him in a crisis involving the Sun-Eater, while Green Lantern: Rebirth eventually reframes his fall as possession by the fear entity. Read those as subsequent developments, not explanations the 1994 event itself supplied.
