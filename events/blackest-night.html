Blackest Night

The dead rise. The light must unite.

IssuesBlackest Night #0–8
PublishedJune 2009 – May 2010
WriterGeoff Johns
ArtistIvan Reis
SourcesPublication details via Wikipedia

Continuity note: The culmination of the "War of Light" prophecy seeded in Green Lantern #25 (2007). Black Hand (first appearing in Green Lantern #29, 1964) becomes the first Black Lantern and Nekron's herald. The event's aftermath leads directly into Brightest Day (2010–11).

The story

The prophecy has come due. Black Hand, the Corps' oldest enemy, becomes the herald of Nekron — Lord of the Unliving — and across the universe the dead claw their way back as Black Lanterns, wearing the faces of fallen heroes, friends, and family.

No single Corps can stand against death itself. So the wielders of every color of the emotional spectrum — rage, avarice, fear, will, hope, compassion, love — are forced to fight side by side, in the largest alliance the universe has ever seen.

At the heart of the darkness burns a secret: the white light of life itself, and the Entity that embodies it. The war for the universe will be decided not by the strongest ring, but by the light that unites them all.
