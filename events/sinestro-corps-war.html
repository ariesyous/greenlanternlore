Sinestro Corps War

Fear declares war on will.

IssuesGreen Lantern: Sinestro Corps Special #1, Green Lantern #21–25, Green Lantern Corps #14–19
PublishedJune – December 2007
WriterGeoff Johns, Dave Gibbons, Peter Tomasi
ArtistIvan Reis, Patrick Gleason, Ethan Van Sciver
SourcesPublication details via Wikipedia

Continuity note: Sinestro was believed killed by Hal Jordan in Emerald Twilight (1994); Green Lantern: Rebirth (2004–05) later revealed that death was a Parallax-created illusion. This event also introduces the "War of Light" — the prophecy that the seven corps will war, leading directly to Blackest Night.

The story

Sinestro has re-emerged — presumed dead since Emerald Twilight — and he hasn't come alone. Forging thousands of yellow power rings fueled by fear, he builds the Sinestro Corps in the Corps' mirror image, then allies it with nightmares: the Anti-Monitor, a Parallax-possessed Kyle Rayner, Superboy-Prime, and the Cyborg Superman.

The war ignites across the universe — from the antimatter world of Qward to the Guardians' homeworld of Oa — and crashes down on Earth itself. Outnumbered and outgunned, the Green Lantern Corps is pushed to the brink of extinction, and the Guardians are forced to rewrite the ancient laws of the Book of Oa just to survive.

It is the first true war of light, and it ends with a revelation: the Guardians' own prophecy speaks of more corps to come — and a war of light that will consume everything.

Aftermath

The war cracks the prophecy of the Blackest Night wide open. In its wake, the red, orange, blue, indigo, violet, and black corps all rise — the War of Light has begun.
