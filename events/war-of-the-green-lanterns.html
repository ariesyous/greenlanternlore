War of the Green Lanterns

The Corps turns its rings on itself.

Continuity note: The final Green Lantern event before DC's 2011 New 52 relaunch. Unlike most titles, the Green Lantern books carried their continuity forward — the New 52 Green Lantern #1 picks up directly from this story's cliffhanger.

The story

Krona — the renegade Guardian whose obsession with the universe's origins once unleashed evil itself — makes his final play. Seizing the Central Power Battery, he imprisons the emotional entities within it and turns every Green Lantern in the universe into his mind-controlled army.

Every Lantern, that is, except four. Hal Jordan, Guy Gardner, John Stewart, and Kyle Rayner slip the net — and go rogue, arming themselves with rings of the other corps: yellow, red, blue, violet, indigo, orange. To save the Corps, they must fight the Corps.

Their target: Oa itself, the Battery, and the entities raging inside it — Parallax, Ion, Ophidian, and the rest. The war for the green light will be won by Lanterns wearing every other color.

Aftermath

The entities are freed and Krona is defeated — but the Guardians' authority lies in ruins. Their desperate answer, the Third Army, will nearly destroy everything they built.
