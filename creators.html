Behind the Scenes

The
Creators

The writers and artists who forged the mythos — the hands behind the rings.

Writer — the modern saga

Geoff Johns

The architect of the modern Green Lantern mythos. His nine-year run (2004–2013) — from Rebirth through Blackest Night — created the emotional spectrum, the nine corps, and the saga this site is built around. No one has shaped the franchise more.

Artists — the modern saga

Ethan Van Sciver & Ivan Reis

The definitive visual voices of the Johns era. Van Sciver's hyper-detailed Rebirth and Sinestro Corps War set the tone; Reis's Blackest Night gave the saga its epic scale. Their designs — the corps uniforms, the entities — are the ones you picture.

Writer & artist — the Kyle Rayner era

Ron Marz & Darryl Banks

When Hal Jordan fell in Emerald Twilight (1994), Marz and Banks built what came next: Kyle Rayner, the Torchbearer, carrying the last ring alone. Their decade-long run kept the franchise alive through its darkest years.

Writer & artist — "Tygers"

Alan Moore & Kevin O'Neill

In a single 1986 annual story, Moore and O'Neill gave the Corps its greatest prophecy — naming Sodam Yat the ultimate Lantern — and deepened the mythology more in 40 pages than most runs manage in years.

Writer & artist — Hard Traveling Heroes

Denny O'Neil & Neal Adams

In 1970–71, O'Neil and Adams took Hal Jordan off-world and onto America's highways, pairing him with Green Arrow for socially conscious stories that proved superhero comics could grapple with real issues. The run that made Green Lantern matter.

Writer & artist — the Silver Age

John Broome & Gil Kane

The creators of Hal Jordan himself. From Showcase #22 (1959), Broome's cosmic imagination and Kane's dynamic, elongated figures defined the Silver Age Green Lantern — Sinestro, the Guardians, Oa, and the Corps itself all started here.

Writer & artist — The Green Lantern

Grant Morrison & Liam Sharp

Morrison's 2019–21 run reimagined Hal Jordan as a cosmic beat cop in a psychedelic police procedural — the most divisive and discussed GL run in years. Love it or hate it, nobody else writes Lanterns like this.
