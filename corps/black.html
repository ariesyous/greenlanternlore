Death

Black Lantern Corps

Not a color but the absence of one. The black light doesn't feel — it hungers.

The Oath (recited by Black Hand)

The Blackest Night falls from the skies, the darkness grows as all light dies. We crave your hearts and your demise, by my black hand, the dead shall rise!

The Emotion

Death — or more precisely, the absence of emotion. Black is not a color of the spectrum; it is the void the spectrum shines against.

Black Lanterns don't feel rage or love or fear. They consume feeling — ripping the emotional hearts from the living to feed the dark.

Base of Operations

Nowhere — and everywhere the dead lie. The Black Lanterns have no homeworld, no sectors, no Central Battery as others know it.

They are led by Black Hand, avatar of Nekron, Lord of the Unliving — death's god, older than the universe's first light.

Notable Members

  • Black Hand — the first Black Lantern; death's herald and Nekron's avatar.
  • Nekron — Lord of the Unliving; the hunger behind the rings.
  • The Risen — the reanimated dead of the universe, wearing the faces of the fallen.

Their names are left unspoken here — some stories are still being read.

The Ring

A black power ring reanimates the dead, restoring the corpse and downloading a copy of the deceased's memories — then sets it hunting the living. It reads emotional auras like a menu.

Quirks: black rings are nearly indestructible — only the lights of the spectrum, combined to recreate the white light of life, can unmake them. And every ring speaks one command to the grave: "Rise."

Lore

Every Corps on this site fears the same prophecy: the Blackest Night, when death itself would rise against all light and all emotion. The Guardians buried the prophecy. The Zamarons whispered it. The Indigo Tribe prepared for it.

The Black Lanterns are that prophecy wearing a ring — the universe's reminder that every light casts a shadow, and the shadow has been waiting longer than any of them.
