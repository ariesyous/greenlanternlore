Life

White Lantern Corps

Every color, combined. The white light is life itself — and it chooses its bearers.

Image: DC Comics via ComicBook.com — fan tribute, not affiliated.

The Oath

No formal oath has ever been revealed.

The white light is too rare, and its bearers too few, for a creed to have been written down.

The Emotion

Life — the white light is not one emotion but all seven colors of the spectrum fused into one: rage, avarice, fear, will, hope, compassion, and love, burning together.

It is the hardest light to wield and the rarest to see. It does not recruit. It chooses.

Base of Operations

None. There is no homeworld of the white light, no sectors, no Central Battery.

The light manifests where it wills — most often through the Life Entity, the living embodiment of life in the universe, which has hidden at the heart of the Earth itself.

Notable Bearers

Kyle Rayner— the one Lantern to master the entire spectrum and wield the white light as his own. T The Life Entity— life incarnate; the source the white light flows from.

The Ring

A white power ring commands the full spectrum at once — every Corps' abilities in a single band of light. It can heal any wound, purge any corruption of the other lights, and even return the dead to life.

Quirks: the ring is sentient in its choosing and abandons bearers it deems unworthy. Great power, zero job security.

Lore

Every light on this site is a fragment. The white light is the whole: in the beginning, the universe's first life sparked the emotional spectrum into being, and white is what the spectrum looks like when nothing is divided.

It appears only at the moments that matter most — when the lights stop fighting each other long enough to remember they were once one light. That is why there is no oath. Some things are too big for four lines.
