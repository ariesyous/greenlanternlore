Fear

Sinestro Corps

Order through terror — an army built on the one emotion the Green Lanterns were never meant to touch.

Composite using Sinestro foreground from Green Lantern: Sinestro Corps Special #1 (2007), story page 4. Ethan Van Sciver (foreground illustration); Moose Baumann (foreground colors). Artwork source & details.

The Oath

In blackest day, in brightest night, beware your fears made into light. Let those who try to stop what's right, burn like my power — Sinestro's might!

The Emotion

Fear — the yellow light feeds on terror: the bearer's ability to inspire it, and the universe's endless supply of it. Where willpower pushes through fear, the yellow light weaponizes it.

A yellow ring seeks out those who can instill great fear in others. Its wielders don't conquer despite fear — they rule because of it.

Base of Operations

Qward — a bleak world in the antimatter universe, a dark mirror of Oa. Its native Weaponers forged some of the most dangerous artifacts in existence.

From Qward, the Sinestro Corps projects fear across the positive-matter universe, sector by sector.

Notable Members

Sinestro— founder; once the Green Lanterns' greatest, now fear's most eloquent preacher. Arkillo— the Corps' brutal drill instructor; a mountain of muscle and menace. Lyssa Drak— keeper of the Book of Parallax; the Corps' historian and prophet. Karu-Sil— raised by beasts; hunts alongside a pack of living constructs. Despotellis— a sentient virus; fear at the microscopic level.

The Ring

A yellow power ring mirrors the Green Lanterns' capabilities — hard-light constructs shaped by the bearer's mind — but every creation is tuned to inspire dread.

Quirks: the ring's charge is drawn from the yellow Central Power Battery on Qward. Its great strength is also its tell: the Corps' power grows wherever fear spreads, and withers where courage holds.

Lore

Thaal Sinestro was the Green Lantern Corps' finest — until his methods curdled into tyranny and the Guardians cast him out. Exiled to the antimatter universe, he found the Qwardians and a terrible idea: if willpower could be forged into a Corps, so could fear.

The Sinestro Corps was built as a dark reflection of everything the Green Lanterns stand for — same structure, same discipline, opposite light. Its existence forced the universe to confront an uncomfortable truth the Guardians had buried: that fear had always been the flaw in the green light.

Key Stories

The Sinestro Corps War (2007) — Sinestro's fear-powered army, built in the antimatter universe of Qward, launches its invasion. The Corps' defining war.

Sinestro's Fall (Green Lantern #7, 1961) — The greatest Green Lantern becomes a dictator on Korugar, is stripped of his ring, and exiled to Qward — where he forges the yellow ring.

War of the Green Lanterns (2011) — Krona's madness turns the Corps against itself, and the yellow light plays a pivotal role in the chaos.

Sinestro's Solo Series (Sinestro #1–23, 2014–16) — Sinestro rebuilds his Corps as a force for order, wrestling with whether fear can ever truly serve justice.
