Where life itself sleeps — the White Lantern's cradle.

Earth

Every world on this list is defined by the Corps that claims it. Earth is different. Earth doesn't belong to the White Lanterns — the White Lanterns belong to Earth.

Billions of years ago, the Guardians discovered something buried deep in Earth's crust: the Entity, the living embodiment of the white light of creation — life itself. They hid it there, lied about it, told the universe that life began on Oa. But the truth was always here, sleeping beneath our feet. When Nekron and the Black Lanterns came during Blackest Night, they came for the Entity. To kill it would be to kill everything.

And so, on Earth, the White Lantern Corps was born. Sinestro bonded with the Entity first — "Thaal Sinestro of Korugar, destiny awaits" — then Hal Jordan, then the resurrected heroes: Superman, Wonder Woman, the Flash, and more. The image above captures the moment: "Let there be light." Twelve white rings, twelve resurrected champions, one last stand against death itself. After Nekron's defeat, a White Lantern Power Battery remained on Earth. It's still here. And the Entity? It sleeps again. But now we know where to find it.

First appearanceBlackest Night #7 (2010) — the Entity bonds with Sinestro
Notable featuresThe Entity (the embodiment of life), the White Central Power Battery
StatusActive — the Entity slumbers; the Battery remains
