Nok

Nok barely exists on any star chart, and the Indigo Tribe likes it that way. This dense jungle world is where the indigo light was first harnessed — where Natromo the Nok native forged the first indigo staff, and where the Tribe brings the universe's worst criminals to be... rehabilitated.

The Tribe's methods are the spectrum's darkest secret: indigo rings don't recruit, they conscript, forcing compassion into sociopaths and murderers. Nok's jungles hide the conversion rituals, the staff-forges, and the truth about what the Tribe really is. Even the Guardians tread carefully here. Some lights are better left unexamined.

First appearanceGreen Lantern (vol. 4) #38 (2009)
Notable featuresThe indigo Central Power Battery, the conversion groves
StatusActive — hidden home of the Indigo Tribe
