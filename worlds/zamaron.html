Zamaron

Zamaron is Oa's sister world in more ways than one. Its rulers, the Zamarons — the female counterparts of the Guardians — abandoned the Guardians' cold logic millennia ago, choosing instead to harness the violet light of love. Their world is beautiful and terrible: crystalline spires rising from a landscape shaped by emotion itself.

Here the Star Sapphire Corps is forged. The Zamarons believe love is the universe's most powerful force — and its most dangerous. They don't recruit; they conscript, imprisoning those whose hearts burn brightest inside violet crystals until they accept the ring. Carol Ferris, Hal Jordan's great love, became their queen — proof that on Zamaron, love and war are the same thing.

First appearanceGreen Lantern (vol. 2) #16 (1962)
Notable featuresThe violet Central Power Battery, the crystal conversion chambers
StatusActive — seat of the Star Sapphires
