Lanterns (HBO)

The biggest Green Lantern screen story ever — Hal and John, veteran and rookie.

2026

Live-action series

Where to watch: Streaming on Max.

The story

Eight episodes. Hal Jordan — the legend, older now — and John Stewart — the rookie — investigating a mystery on Earth that pulls them into the wider DC universe. From showrunner Chris Mundy, with Damon Lindelof and Tom King among its architects, Lanterns was conceived as a grounded, character-driven take: True Detective with power rings.

It became the biggest DC Studios premiere in HBO Max history — the largest Green Lantern screen story ever by every measure, and the project that finally made the Corps work in live action by leading with character instead of spectacle.

For comics readers, it's a fascinating mirror: the show draws on Secret Origin, the Johns era, and John's own history while building something new. Start with the comics it echoes — then argue about the differences.
