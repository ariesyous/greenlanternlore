The story

Lanterns pairs experienced Lantern Hal Jordan, played by Kyle Chandler, with new recruit John Stewart, played by Aaron Pierre. A murder investigation in the American heartland draws them into a mystery with cosmic connections. The eight-episode first season is led by showrunner Chris Mundy, with Damon Lindelof and Tom King as fellow co-creators.

HBO's series announcement establishes the premise and creative team; its July 2026 programming release identifies the cast, eight-episode season, and August 16 debut.

For a comics introduction, try Secret Origin for Hal or our War Journal guide for John. These are reading recommendations, not a claim that either book supplies the show's plot. The continuity guide explains why screen and comics histories should be read separately.
