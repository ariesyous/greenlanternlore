On Screen · Animated series

Justice League / Justice League Unlimited

For a generation of fans, John Stewart was Green Lantern.

2001 – 2006

Animated series

Where to watch: Streaming on Max; Blu-ray box sets.

The story

Before the live-action boom, before the streaming era, there was the DC Animated Universe — and its Green Lantern was John Stewart. Voiced with gravel-voiced authority by Phil LaMarr, Stewart was the Justice League's steady center: a former Marine, an architect, a man who treated the most powerful weapon in the universe like a responsibility rather than a gift.

Across five seasons, JLU gave John Stewart the richest characterization any Lantern had received on screen: his military discipline, his romance with Hawkgirl, his clashes with a future self, his role as the League's conscience. For millions of viewers, this is the definitive Green Lantern — the version that made John Stewart a household name and cemented him as Hal Jordan's equal in the public imagination.

The show's influence runs deep: its version of Stewart shaped the comics' take on the character for years, and its tone — serious, character-driven, unafraid of moral complexity — set the template for DC animation that followed.

At a glance

  • Format: Animated series, 91 episodes
  • Voices: Phil LaMarr as John Stewart
  • Showrunners: Bruce Timm, Dwayne McDuffie
