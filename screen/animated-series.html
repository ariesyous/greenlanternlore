Green Lantern: The Animated Series

A space-western on the universe's far frontier — the Corps' best screen outing.

2011 – 2013

Animated series

Where to watch: Streaming on Max; digital purchase.

The story

Set on the far edge of Guardian space, GL:TAS is a space-western: Hal Jordan and Kilowog patrol the frontier in the Interceptor, joined by Razer — a Red Lantern questioning his rage — and the ship's AI, Aya.

It's the most comics-faithful Lantern show ever made: the emotional spectrum, the Red Lanterns, the Manhunters, the Star Sapphires, all woven into a serialized story about what the Corps means when no one's watching. Razer's arc — from rage to something like redemption — is the show's soul, and one of the best character arcs in DC animation.

Cancelled after one season (26 episodes) despite critical love, it remains the high-water mark: proof that the Lantern mythos sings when it's allowed to be cosmic, weird, and sincere.

At a glance

  • Format: CGI animated series, 26 episodes
  • Voices: Josh Keaton (Hal), Kevin Michael Richardson (Kilowog)
  • Showrunner: Giancarlo Volpe
