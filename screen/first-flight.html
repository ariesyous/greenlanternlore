On Screen · Animated film

Green Lantern: First Flight

Hal Jordan's origin retold — a rookie's first mission, a mentor's betrayal.

2009

Animated film

Where to watch: Streaming on Max; Blu-ray / digital.

The story

The first DC Universe Animated Original Movie to star a Lantern, First Flight retells Hal Jordan's origin with the confidence of a franchise that knows exactly what it is. Hal is the rookie; Sinestro is the legendary mentor; and the lesson — that the Corps' greatest hero might also be its greatest threat — lands like a hammer.

What makes it sing is Sinestro. Long before the live-action film fumbled him, First Flight gave us the definitive screen Sinestro: principled, terrifying, utterly convinced that fear is the only order the universe respects. His turn is the film's engine, and it remains the best villain performance in any Lantern adaptation.

At 77 minutes it's a tight, propulsive ride — and still the best entry point for anyone who wants the Hal/Sinestro dynamic in one sitting.

At a glance

  • Format: Animated film, 77 min
  • Voices: Christopher Meloni (Hal), Victor Garber (Sinestro)
  • Director: Lauren Montgomery
