On Screen · Animated anthology film

Green Lantern: Emerald Knights

Six tales of the Corps, from the first Lantern to the present day.

2011

Animated anthology film

Where to watch: Streaming on Max; Blu-ray / digital.

The story

An anthology film with a frame story: as Krona's shadow falls, Hal Jordan tells the rookie Arisia Rrab six tales of the Corps — and each one is a window into a different corner of the mythos.

The lineup is a fan's mixtape: the first Lantern, Avra; Kilowog's brutal training days; Laira's tragic fall; Mogo's legend; Abin Sur's fate; and Emerald Knights' own climax. It's the rare adaptation that treats the Corps as an institution with history — not just Hal Jordan's supporting cast.

For lore-hounds, it's essential: the Laira segment in particular is the character's best screen outing, and the film's respect for deep continuity set it apart from every Lantern project before it.

At a glance

  • Format: Animated anthology, 84 min
  • Voices: Nathan Fillion (Hal), Elisabeth Moss (Arisia)
  • Directors: Christopher Berkeley, Lauren Montgomery
